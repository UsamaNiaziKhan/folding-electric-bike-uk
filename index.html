Folding Electric Bike UK: The Commuter's Best Friend

A folding electric bike solves the two biggest problems of UK commuting: storage and the last mile. It folds down in seconds to fit a car boot, a flat hallway or under a desk, and the motor flattens hills and headwinds on the ride in. For train commuters, flat dwellers and anyone short on space, folding is the format that makes an e-bike practical every single day.

Battery and Range: Buy for Your Longest Ride

The golden rule is simple: take your longest regular round trip, double it, and buy the battery for that number. Cold weather, hills and heavier riders all eat range, so headroom matters. A 36V 10Ah battery suits 30 to 40 km of real-world riding, while 48V and 52V packs stretch past 80 km.
